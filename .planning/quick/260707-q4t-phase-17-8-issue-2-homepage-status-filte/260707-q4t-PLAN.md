Effective status values (computed client-side):
- `"active"` — on-chain Active AND current_block < deadline_block
- `"expired_success"` — on-chain Active AND current_block >= deadline_block AND funded
- `"expired_failed"` — on-chain Active AND current_block >= deadline_block AND NOT funded
- `"success"` — on-chain Success (finalized with goal reached)
- `"failed"` — on-chain Failed (finalized below goal)

Filter tabs map:
- `All` — show all campaigns
- `Active` — effectiveStatus === "active"
- `Funded` — effectiveStatus === "success"
- `Unsuccessful` — effectiveStatus === "failed"
- `Expired` — effectiveStatus === "expired_success" OR "expired_failed"
